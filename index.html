ClipTool

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels.

ClipTool is a personal, non-commercial tool. It runs on the owner's own computer and uploads short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) that the owner prepared to YouTube channels that the owner manages.

What it does with Google / YouTube

  • Uploads videos to the channel the owner selects during Google sign-in (YouTube Data API, upload scope).
  • Reads that channel's name and the view, like and comment counts of the videos it uploaded (read-only scope).

It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.

Privacy policy

ClipTool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
